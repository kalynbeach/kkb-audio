## Implementation and exact limits

`LocalMp3` owns one completed encoded anchor and at most one transient replacement. The
replacement is acquired through the unchanged strict linear decoder before loop readiness;
initial head/start reconstruction may remain linear. `PreparedMediaLoop` publishes the exact
converter source anchor for leaving its4096-frame head. Native and browser hosts prepare that
selected continuation before publishing head/four-slot readiness. Normal head exits restore
that recipe without a new scan, public seek, epoch or render instance.

The recipe stores raw/source/byte coordinates, unchanged predecessor, genuine≤511-byte
main-data suffix and original unread-reservoir bookkeeping. Reset; decode a same-rate/channel
internal zero-side-information carrier; decode the original predecessor; discard both outputs.
Only then expose the target packet at `converter_anchor + validated_delay`, discarding its
within-packet prefix. Actual beginning and packet1 use their shorter zero/one-packet paths.
Carrier PCM is never media, waveform input, metadata, source trim or validation evidence.

The finite horizon is derived from pinned Symphonia0.6.1 source, not guessed preroll:

- [MPEG-1 side information](https://docs.rs/symphonia-bundle-mp3/0.6.1/src/symphonia_bundle_mp3/layer3/bitstream.rs.html):9-bit reservoir reference; SCFSI reuse stays inside one packet.
- [Reservoir and Layer3 processing](https://docs.rs/symphonia-bundle-mp3/0.6.1/src/symphonia_bundle_mp3/layer3/mod.rs.html): exact suffix selection and consumed-byte tracking; fresh per-packet side information.
- [Hybrid synthesis](https://docs.rs/symphonia-bundle-mp3/0.6.1/src/symphonia_bundle_mp3/layer3/hybrid_synthesis.rs.html): each granule replaces overlap, including long/short/mixed/zero branches.
- [Synthesis FIFO](https://docs.rs/symphonia-bundle-mp3/0.6.1/src/symphonia_bundle_mp3/synthesis.rs.html):16 steps; the predecessor's second granule supplies18 correct steps after the first restores hybrid overlap.

Original predecessor/target CRC and genuine reservoir checks are still mandatory. Carrier
padding cannot legitimize missing source history. Decoder/I/O errors remain terminal track
failures. The decoder dependency, initial whole-file validation, trim/metadata policy and
supported rates/layouts/file limits are unchanged. Ordinary same-rate MP3 seeks still report
`AnchorAndDiscard`; grid adjustment remains explicit. No recovered encoder-input claim.

### Storage and ownership

Fixed encoded arrays per anchor:1045 predecessor +511 history =1556bytes. At most two anchor
records,511-byte rolling acquisition history,1044-byte carrier scratch and one1045-byte owned
packet-input copy give a conservative additional encoded-payload ceiling of **5712bytes**.
The acquisition object is boxed to keep the private reader enum compact. No decoder pool or
per-frame index is retained. Tests inspect fixed anchor array sizes, cancellation and bounded
zero/one/two warm-up counts. The representation, not a favorable observed track size, enforces
the payload bound.

This is not total process memory: existing host64KiB browser encoded cache (including its
existing replacement transient), native6KiB read scratch, decoder2048-byte reservoir,
spectral/overlap/synthesis and output buffers, converter working memory, object/scalar/allocator
overhead are separate. Carrier and packet copies are sequentially retired off-callback.
Converted head≤4096, four1024-frame slots and fixed16,777,216-byte Wasm memory remain unchanged.
No callback decoding, waits, allocation or ownership destruction was added.

Superseding preparation cancels pending acquisition before it can install/publish; current
request checks bracket asynchronous reads and bounded restores. Completed immutable same-owner/
same-target recipes can be reused across verified requests. Source replacement/close releases
all state. Shared #19 Disabled/Preparing/Armed/Active/Failed, exact acknowledgment-time PCM/EOS,
paused enable, cancelled intent, inert disabled edits, held-head smoothing, fixed output period,
quantization drift and LoopUnderrun recovery contracts are unchanged.
